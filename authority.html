How the system works

Four stages. One repeating loop.

01 — CAPTURE
A single 60-minute studio session.
Once a month, you walk into the studio. We handle audio, video, lighting, prep questions. You walk out 60 minutes later with the raw material for an entire month of content.
02 — PRODUCE
Editorial team turns raw into release-ready.
Cut the episode, master the audio, design the cover, draft the show notes, build the visual identity. The episode is the spine — everything else extends from it.
03 — MULTIPLY
One episode becomes thirty pieces.
Clips. Reels. Quote cards. Carousels. Articles. Newsletter. Each piece is hand-cut, branded, and built for the platform it'll live on. No generic AI slop, no batch-spinner mush.
04 — DISTRIBUTE
Published on a calendar that runs without you.
Spotify, Apple, YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, your newsletter — every platform on its own cadence. You stay focused on the work that earned you the audience in the first place.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is one studio session a month really enough?
Yes. A 60-minute episode contains 60 minutes of insight, dozens of quotable moments, multiple sub-topics, and natural breaks. Our editorial team is trained to identify and extract every piece of content that's already there.
Do you write the content for me?
We adapt your spoken content into written form — articles, posts, captions. They're written in your voice (because they're literally your words). You review and approve before anything publishes.
How long until I see results?
Production starts immediately. You'll have your first episode + content batch live within 2 weeks. Audience compounds over the first 90 days — that's where most clients see the inflection.
What if I already have a podcast?
Even better. We can absorb your existing show, take over production, and start applying the multiplication system to existing back-catalog episodes. Most clients see immediate gains from re-publishing old episodes as new derivatives.
Can I do this without the studio?
If you have your own setup, yes — we can adapt. But the studio matters: production quality is non-negotiable for the kind of authority compounding we deliver.
