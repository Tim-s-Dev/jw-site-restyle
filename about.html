A letter from Tim & Mel

Dear founder,

We started JourneyWell in 2022 because we kept watching the gap between DIY content and agency content eat founders alive. Doing it yourself meant nights, weekends, and a show that quietly died at episode six. Hiring an agency meant six month timelines, hostage files, and content that sounded like nobody in particular.

So we built the thing in between. A real studio with a real editorial team, kept small on purpose: small enough to know your voice, your clients, and what you actually sell, systemized enough to ship every single week.

Four years in, that is still the whole model. A husband and wife in the middle of it, a handful of editors and producers we trust completely, and a calendar that does not slip. We would rather stay this size and be essential to fifteen brands than triple and be forgettable to a hundred.

Come see the room sometime. The coffee is good and the mics are always hot.

See you in the studio,

Tim & Melodie

How we work

Four operating principles.

In order of how often we say them out loud.

i.

Done, not perfect.

A shipped episode beats a perfect plan, and polish without cadence is a graveyard.

ii.

You own everything.

Raw files, masters, feeds, accounts: all yours from day one, because we are allergic to hostage taking.

iii.

It has to sound like you.

Everything we ship is cut from your actual voice and words, and if your mom would not recognize you in it, we redo it.

iv.

Real numbers, real reviews.

Monthly reporting on what actually moved: impressions, listens, replies, booked calls. No vanity decks.

Where we are

Baton Rouge, Louisiana.

A real room you can walk into. Mics hot, lights set, coffee on. Most of our clients are Louisiana founders who record with us monthly, and remote founders fly in to batch an entire quarter in a single day.
