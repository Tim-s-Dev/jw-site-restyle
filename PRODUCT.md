# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and operators of small-to-mid companies who need professional video content — podcasts, founder sessions, short-form social — and want to walk into a real studio in Baton Rouge, sit down on a Monday, and walk out with shipped assets by Friday. They've been burned by agencies who promise decks, and by hiring in-house with a Sony A7 that never gets used. They want to film, not to brief.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for JourneyWell, a working video production studio. The site exists to make a founder say "that's the room I want to be in" and book a session. Success = booked studio time and qualified leads through the n8n form fan-out. Secondary surfaces (portfolio, blog, About) exist to reinforce that the studio is real, the team ships, and the work proves itself without adjectives.

## Brand Personality

Confident · editorial · hands-on. A founder talking to another founder, not an agency pitching a client. Plain copy, big imagery, no superlatives. Lime is the brand's signature — a statement, not a decoration. Apple Garamond italics carry the editorial voice; Helvetica Now Display carries the loud one. The brand sounds like someone who's done the work, not someone selling the idea of it.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate-stiff agency sites.** Navy + grey palette, stock smiling-team photos, jargon-heavy taglines ("we deliver impactful narratives that resonate"), portfolio sections that hide the work behind hover states. JW is the opposite: real studio photos, plain language, work visible immediately.
- Generic SaaS-cream warm-neutral body backgrounds with tiny tracked eyebrows on every section. AI-default cosplay of editorial restraint.
- Hype-y crypto/startup-loud gradient hero pages with glassmorphism cards.
- Anything that reads as "branding language about branding." If a sentence could be on any other agency site, cut it.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the room.** Every key page should make the studio feel physical: lights, monitors, mics, founders sitting down. Photography carries weight that copy can't fake.
2. **Founder-to-founder voice.** Direct, peer-level, no client/vendor distance. "We" and "you" sit at the same table.
3. **Made, not promised.** Proof beats adjectives. The portfolio, the studio shots, and the daily-shipped blog do the talking. Adjective-stuffed taglines get cut.
4. **Lime is the brand, not the decoration.** The saturated lime/accent green is JourneyWell's signature; it should earn placement (em italics, eyebrows on lite pages, CTAs), not be sprinkled for warmth. In dark mode it shouts; in light mode it deepens to `#169c46`.
5. **Editorial restraint over agency polish.** Confidence comes from type hierarchy, photo crop, whitespace, and a single committed accent — not from gradients, glass, or decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against its background in both themes (lime on cream fails — that's why the em color drops to `#169c46` in light mode, not the bright `#d4ff00`). Focus rings visible on dark and cream backgrounds. Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Photo grids have meaningful alt text describing what's in the shot (the room, the camera, the founder), not just "studio image."
