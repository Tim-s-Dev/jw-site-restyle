FAQ

Common questions.

Two weeks really? What's the catch?
No catch — but we move fast. We've done this 15+ times. The system is documented. We don't redesign workshops; we ship episodes. The "catch" is that you have to be available for the day-1 session and decisive on the brand picks.
What if I have a name and concept already?
Even better — we can compress to 7–10 days. The first conversation tells us how much pre-work is left to do.
Do I have to do every episode in the studio?
For best quality, yes. But we offer remote-guest sessions and even hybrid setups. Talk it through with us — most setups work.
Can I keep my existing podcast feed?
Yes. We migrate the RSS feed so subscribers don't lose access. Existing episodes stay where they are; new ones publish under our system.
What happens if I want to cancel?
Month-to-month after the first 90 days. You keep all your masters, brand assets, and feeds. We don't hold anything hostage.
