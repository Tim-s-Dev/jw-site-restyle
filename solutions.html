FAQ

Common questions.

If yours isn't here, just ask in the drawer.

Do I have to commit to a year?

No. Studio is per-session. Podcast Launch and Authority System are month-to-month after the first quarter. We earn the renewal.

What if I already have a podcast?

Common. We migrate it. We've taken over feeds, rebranded shows, fixed distribution, and rebuilt back catalogs for clients who started elsewhere.

Do you write the content for me?

We help structure each recording (questions, arc, hooks). The voice is always yours. Captions, headlines, and supporting copy are produced by our editorial team and reviewed before anything ships.

What platforms do you publish to?

Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, your blog, your email — wherever your audience is. We tailor format per platform, not just upload the same file everywhere.

Can I see real numbers from existing clients?

Yes — on a call. We don't post case studies with real performance data publicly because most clients prefer it that way.
