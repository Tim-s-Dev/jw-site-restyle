FAQ

Common questions.

How far in advance do I need to book?
Most weeks have openings within 5–7 days. For specific dates, 2–3 weeks ahead is safest. Submit the form and we'll confirm same-day.
What if I just need to record one episode?
No problem. Single-session bookings are common. We don't push you into a retainer unless it's clearly the right fit.
Can I bring a guest who's flying in?
Yes. We can also do remote-guest sessions where one or more participants Zoom in with high-quality remote capture. Just flag it on the form.
Do I have to also use you for editing?
No. You can take the raw masters and edit anywhere. That said, most clients who book the studio also book the editing — the workflow is much faster end-to-end.
Where is the studio?
Baton Rouge, Louisiana. We'll send the address with your booking confirmation.
